# KBC One v9: Learn the road. Build your village.

**Goal on screen: “Build your village by learning from people ahead and preparing the road before you drive it.”**

The player changes the world with knowledge. A road roller fills a bump. A crane places a roof. Windows illuminate. The car returns home through the street the player just improved.

## 1. The first 5 seconds

Home has one-tap entry: **Your village · Prepare your move**. Keep the phone rotation into landscape, screen power-on, and camera descent. Reveal a small Belgian village, its Everyday ring, and one highlighted avenue. Show the goal immediately, including during rotation.

```
 BUILD YOUR VILLAGE                         [2D] [Exit]
 Today's build: your home's reading room
 [your home + empty room outline] --- [car] --- ^ bump
 Learn the energy lesson to build this room.
                    [Drive & prepare]
```

The room outline makes the reward desirable before the lesson. The player chooses its position and roof colour after earning it. There is no construction timer, inventory, currency, or store.

## 2. One stop: 30 seconds, without a countdown

The world remains visible behind one short card. Each step needs one tap; reading can take longer.

| Time | Player action | Feedback |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 s: guess | Choose between 2 answers, or “Show me”. | Place a prediction marker; no correctness score. |
| 4–9 s: reveal | Compare the guess with one published crowd fact. | The marker meets the evidence; show its meaning. |
| 9–15 s: what helped | Inspect one relevant action; expand for the ranked list. | Explain the association and show a short crowd note. |
| 15–24 s: apply | Solve a new situation and select its reason. | The preparation changes the practice road. |
| 24–30 s: save | Save a reminder, checklist, or question; “Not now” stays visible. | Confirm the saved item separately; assemble the earned room. |

The application tests a principle, not recall of a percentage. An incorrect answer gets a concrete explanation and a different example. Help is always available. Completing a supported example earns the build; the passport records **Practised with help**. Independent application earns **Explained now**. Neither guessing correctly nor saving a product earns extra progress.

## 3. One session: a 3-minute construction expedition

Choose one destination and preview its build. Drive a short segment, prepare 2 stops, then return home. Hold to drive; release to brake. Steering is optional, stops catch the car automatically, and “Take me there” supports touch users. Skill comes from preparation, not driving precision.

Ghost cars pass ahead and pull into the upcoming stop: a visible invitation to learn from earlier travellers. Use only `roadViewFor().ghostGroups`, respecting each count/density gate. Label them **Illustrative groups · synthetic demo data**. They are not individual customers, trajectories, or evidence of preparation causing success. Suppressed groups produce no cars or inferred density.

Each solved application paves its practice bump. Each distinct lesson adds one named building part. Completing the session's 2 lessons lights that avenue and opens the camera for placement. Other roads remain available; replaying the same answer cannot generate more buildings.

An unprepared bump gently lifts the bonnet and stops the car. **“Let's try that again. What was missing?”** Rewind 5 seconds, change preparation, and replay the identical situation. No lives, damage, lost buildings, shame, or random punishment. Paving means “rehearsed this situation”, never “financially safe”.

## 4. Weeks: a village that remembers

Restore the neighbourhood overview: home, car/garage, savings and investments, pension. Tapping a place opens its actual overview. A renter's home can become as beautiful as an owner's. Lesson-built rooms, gardens, and lights express learning; they do not imply assets, insurance coverage, or investment growth.

| Earned event | Permanent visible result |
|---|---|
| Supported or independent application | Build one room, greenhouse, workshop, or garden; pave that practice stop. |
| Finish a 2-lesson expedition | Illuminate its avenue; stamp its lesson names. |
| Unaided application in a later session | Add an engraved lantern: **Remembered later**. |

Keep stamps per topic: Seen, Practised with help, Explained now, Remembered later. Store the source stop, evidence version, principle, and optional saved action. A reminder saved is not a task completed. A failed recall offers practice; it never removes anything.

One Everyday lap represents a year: renewals, tax, holidays, school, December. Offer the next relevant lesson, not daily chores. Invite recall after 7 days and before confirmed dates, opt-in and at most once weekly. For Tom, combine the 25 October recall with preparation for 1 November. Never invent an energy-bill date from a road month. Absence causes no decay; “Nothing to prepare today” is a successful visit. After a lived event, invite a reviewed note for the next traveller, without invented impact counts.

## 7. Equal learning, optional play

Margaret gets the same evidence, worked examples, application, saves, and recall through large 2D cards. No rotation, steering, ghosts, or forced guesses. An optional village illustration reflects identical progress. ## 8. The 90-second jury proof

0–10: rotate, reveal village, show promised room. 10–20: drive into an unprepared bump. 20–50: complete Tom's energy lesson. 50–65: rewind, apply the preparation, pave and pass. 65–80: crane places the room; juror chooses its position. 80–90: return to portrait and show the saved reminder plus village thumbnail.
