# KBC One v9 — Show director proposal

**Proposal, not an implemented or verified demo.** The game is: explore your village, complete short crowd lessons, and improve your places through preparation. Learning changes the world; driving connects the lessons.

## The 5-minute pitch

Use Tom → Margaret → Nadia. Keep the game inside one uninterrupted 90-second scene. Proposed control labels below are acceptance targets, not claims about existing controls.

The pages line is the requested pitch contrast, not a factual claim that every other bank lacks games. If challenged, say so. Do not claim first-to-market, guaranteed savings, reduced calls, or proven causation.

## The 90-second game moment

Keep Tom on **Moving house**. This is one lesson, not a tour of every district. Elapsed times include clicks and pauses.

| Elapsed | What the juror sees | What gets clicked | Line / direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–8 s | **Wow 1:** portrait phone turns, grows into landscape, screen powers on | Home: **Build your village** once | Speak only the ambition and synthetic-data qualification from the main sheet during the transition. |
| 8–18 s | **Wow 2:** village from above; Home, Car, Savings and Pension; Home outlined; clear goal and 0/3 progress | Hold first frame, then **Start lesson 1** | “This is Tom’s village: what he has, and what he can prepare. Learn three things to improve his Home.” |
| 18–28 s | Camera follows Tom’s car along one short road; a few translucent cars travel ahead; **58.300 movers · synthetic demo cohort** | Hold **Drive** for a short stretch; automatic stop at lesson | “These ghosts represent earlier travellers. With approved real data, the crowd would come from customers who lived this road.” |
| 28–43 s | Road pauses; **At key handover, what helps you check a later energy bill?** Three large choices | Juror chooses **Meter photos**, **Monthly payment**, or **Not sure**; presenter taps | “Take a guess. There is no score to lose.” Allow silence. If needed, offer **Show answer**. |
| 43–55 s | Answer and a meter-photo illustration; one sentence explains readings at handover | **What helped earlier movers?** | “Photograph the meter readings at handover. They give you a record to check against the bill.” |
| 55–68 s | One eligible crowd association with its denominator and synthetic label; reminder preview for handover | **Save reminder** or **Not now** | “This crowd result is an association, not a promise. Tom can save a reminder.” Never invent a percentage for the stage. |
| 68–78 s | **Wow 3:** camera returns above village; Home gains a lit porch and garden; **Lesson 1/3 understood**; separately **Reminder saved** | **Back to village** | “The lesson improved his place. The reminder is preparation; it does not mean the task is done.” |
| 78–90 s | **A note for the next mover**; short editable draft: “Photograph the meters when you get the keys.” | Juror suggests a phrase; edit → **Preview note** | “What would you tell the next person?” Show **Demo preview · awaiting review**, then carry it into the recipient preview after 90 seconds. |

Ghost cars are representative marks, never 58.300 rendered identities, live locations, or individual financial histories. Production crowd evidence and visual density require eligible groups of at least 500. If evidence is withheld, show that state and keep the practical lesson. Synthetic notes are examples, not testimonials. Publication requires moderation; the demo must not fake that approval.

## First landscape frame: the 5-second test

```text
+----------------------------------------------------------+
| Back to app          TOM'S VILLAGE          Plain view    |
| Learn 3 moving-house lessons to improve your Home.        |
| Lessons 0/3                         Next: a lit porch     |
|                                                          |
|       Savings          HOME ★           Pension          |
|       [garden]       [your flat]          [place]         |
|                         |                                |
|                   YOU [car] ---- Moving-house road        |
|          Car                                             |
|                 [ Start lesson 1 ]                       |
|                  Demo world · synthetic data             |
+----------------------------------------------------------+
```

Show the goal, progress, reward, player position and one primary action immediately. Use readable labels and strong contrast. The Home represents Tom’s rented flat; grandeur never represents wealth. Places open his existing overview later, one panel at a time. Do not start with a tutorial wall, dashboard, atlas, joystick instructions or moving camera.

Test with five unfamiliar people: show this frame for 5 seconds, hide it, then ask: “What do you do? What changes? Where do you start?” Target four correct answers out of five for all three questions. Otherwise revise the frame before adding effects.

## Player advocacy and stage discipline

- **Margaret, 70s:** Home opens the plain path in one tap. Use large text, generous buttons, full labels and one next step. Preserve explanation, source, save, undo and adviser access. No rotation, driving or quiz is required. Validate this path with older adults; age alone does not define preferences.
- **Nadia:** ask permission before revealing care details. Offer **Not now** and **Talk to Els**. No game, ghosts, quiz, reward, celebratory animation or progress pressure. Never infer consent from entering Home.
- **Calm:** one question or panel at a time; driving stops during reading. Provide keyboard, touch and guided travel. Reduced motion opens the same static landscape. Sound stays off unless enabled. Exit preserves learning and confirmed saves.
- **Never:** shaming, gambling cues, random prizes, money stakes, leaderboards, streak pressure, penalties for wrong answers, or rewards for purchases and risk. Answer reveal earns the same village progress; declining a reminder never blocks it. Quiz answers never feed credit or sales decisions.
- **Rehearse:** warm assets, reset Tom’s lesson and reminder, check note preview and projector text. Keep Kate’s existing style intact. After a 10-second load failure, show a labelled recorded game sequence and continue live on the plain path. Never call a recording live. Cut extra exploration before cutting the juror’s guess or explanation.
