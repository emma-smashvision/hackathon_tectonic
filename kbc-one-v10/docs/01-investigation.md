## A. Kate adaptive home — strong on *now*

What it does (`src/kbc/`):

- **Home in three zones** (`ui/App.tsx`): fixed core bar (balance, Transfer, Pay, Scan, Cards) → headline → **open-intent card** (one guess of why you opened the app; only `split_bill` is built) → **Ready for you** (max 3 one-tap actions) → **My blocks / For you** (Kate's suggestions rendered in the customer's own style, max 2 a day).
- **Blocks** (`blocks.ts`): 19 catalogue blocks + customer-made trackers. Each block is `view(customer) → BlockView | null` with facts in plain words. Protection blocks (`paid_twice`) are forced in.
- **Styles** (`ui/Blocks.tsx`): Calm (max 5), Cards (8), Dense (10), Large & simple (3). Themes generated by Kate, contrast fixed by `theme.ts`.
- **Quick actions** (`actions.ts`): 13 actions with 3 safety levels, `checkAction()` validates params, contacts and money; `suggest()` pre-fills from habits.
- **Composer** (`compose.ts`): code builds the fact sheet and candidates → Claude picks style, blocks, actions, intent and writes reasons → `finalize()` drops unknown ids, forces protection, enforces limits, never moves the customer's own blocks → rules fallback.
- **Kate** (`kate.ts`, `server/service.ts`): streamed chat; reply + `###` + one proposal (action, layout, tracker, theme), validated by `validateProposal()`. Nothing changes until the customer confirms.
- **Trust**: Why sheet, "Don't suggest this again" (mute), "What KBC knows about me" ledger (told / Kate thinks / said no).
- **Cast** (`personas.ts`, `ui/stage.ts`): Tom 29 (moving to Gent, dinner split), Lina 31 (London, daughter Noor), Margaret 74 (zooms, paid twice), Karim 45 (freelance, VAT), Sofie 34 (house fund with Pieter). Clock: 17 Oct 2026, each with its own time of day.
- **Tests**: `src/kbc/kbc.test.ts`, 22 tests.

What it does not do: anything beyond the next weeks. `coming_up` is a hand-written list per persona (`persona.calendar`). Goals have no arrival date or odds. The only "future" is `leftUntilPayday()`.

Gap found: Kate's reply text is checked by the prompt only ("never invent amounts"). Round one had a code check that is not in the new build: `src/lib/kate/respond.ts` → `parseModelReply()` rejects amounts that are not in the context.

## C. Hindsight v4 — strong on *later*

What it does (`src/engine/`, `src/components/`, `src/game/`):

- **Roads are data** (`roads.ts`): 12 roads (11 live, `heat-pump` pending), each with stops (bump, boost, milestone), an optional crossroads, behaviours with readiness, call reasons and blocked products. Care roads: Separation, Losing a parent, Losing your job.
- **Crowd per road** (`simulate.ts`) and the **crowd gate** (`gate.ts`): no number below 500 people, Laplace noise (ε = 1), rounding, and a decision log.
- **Everyday Road** (`everyday.ts`): 11 stops of the Belgian money year (renewals, energy settlement, holiday pay, tax return, holidays, property tax, school start, tax letter, pension saving, bonus, December). `TODAY` = Sat 17 Oct 2026.
- **Your money replayed** (`lives.ts`, `life.ts`): one household per road. `computeLife(road, journey)` returns account, pots, buffer and **fuel** (months of buffer), **next 30 days** with the lowest point, the next salary, the **next 12 months on both lanes**, goals with **ETA**, and `goalCrowd()` odds ("57% of drivers who started like you…"). `todayPick()` chooses "one thing on the road today". `buildRecap()` makes the salary-day recap.
- **Tips become pots** (`life.ts` → `potSpec()`): prepared bumps are paid from the pot ("paved"), unprepared ones from the account ("hit"). Tests check that money only moves.
- **Phone** (`components/phone/`): tabs Today · Route · Road · Pass it on, plus Stop, Fork, Passport, Lock screen, Recap overlay, postcard toast.
- **Hindsight City** (`src/game/`): the Atlas as a drivable 3D city; the ring road = the Everyday Road (one lap = one year), one avenue per life road, distance = time. Driving your own road moves your month forward; other avenues are test drives.
- **Bank side** (`components/stage/`): Director, "Behind the glass" inspector, Atlas with a 2.3M placement run, Adviser desk with an access log the customer sees.
- **Tests**: 29 (engine 6, life 12, city 10, readability 1) + `npm run check:readability` (23 scenes).

What it does not do: anything in the moment. No transactions, no payments, no Kate, no customisation, no styles. The phone has its own fixed dark design.

Engine limits that matter for a merge:

- The household is keyed by **road**: `lifeFor(roadId)` (unknown → falls back to `first-home`). A person without a life road (most people, most of the time) is not supported.
- One active road per person. When it ends, salary day stops (noted as a known gap in v4).
- Journeys live in the browser; the City and the phone share one React store (`components/store.tsx`, used directly by `src/game/hud/*`).

## Can the City run inside the phone?

Yes, with care. What the code does today (`hindsight-v4/src/game/`):

- **Good**: the R3F canvas fills its parent (`.city-canvas` is `position: absolute; inset: 0`); the HUD is positioned inside `.city`; `WorldLabels.tsx` lays labels out in the coordinates of its own box (`clientWidth`, `getBoundingClientRect()` relative to the root) and avoids `[data-hud]` elements inside the same city; the camera already switches between portrait and landscape from the canvas size (`Player.tsx`: `state.size.width < state.size.height`); keys are window listeners, so they work anywhere; touch pads appear on `(pointer: coarse)`.
- **To change**: `.city` and `.city-loading` take `calc(100dvh - 60px)`; `.flash`, `.prompt`, `.sheet`, `.intro` cap their width with `100vw`; the phone-size HUD rules are `@media (max-width: 860px)` on the viewport.
- **The trap**: R3F and `WorldLabels` measure with `getBoundingClientRect()`, which includes CSS transforms. A rotated or `scale()`d phone would misplace labels and pointer input. So the phone may rotate with a transform only *during* the transition; while you play, the landscape screen must have its real width and height.
