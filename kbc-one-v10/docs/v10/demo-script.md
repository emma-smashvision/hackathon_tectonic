# KBC One v10 — 90-second jury run with Tom

## Before the jury arrives

Run `KBC_AI=off bunx next dev -p 3704` from `kbc-one-v10/`.
Open `http://localhost:3704/?p=tom&reset=1` in desktop Chrome at 1512 × 806 or larger.
The reset clears Tom's mission progress, passport stamps, block upgrades and saved mission reminders. It does not clear other personas or account data. The URL consumes `reset=1` once, so a later reload preserves progress.
Let Home finish loading, then scroll the phone to **Your city · Mission 1/3**. Keep browser zoom at 100%.
Use the map/autopilot controls for a repeatable run. Manual driving also works with WASD or arrow keys.
Sound starts muted. **Sound off** enables a quiet engine hum and pickup/pave tones. Keep it off for the jury unless the room has speakers.

## The run

| Time | Do | Say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–10 s | Show Tom's Home and the **▶ Drive** invitation. | “Tom is moving to Gent. His bank gives him a city to explore, with three short missions from people who moved before him.” |
| 10–18 s | Click **▶ Drive**. Let the phone turn, grow and power on. | “This is one continuous city. Your block sits in the centre. Each road leads to a different life event.” |
| 18–30 s | Drive briefly; show the minimap. Select **GPS · First energy bill**, then **Map · M** → **Autopilot there →**. | “You can take any road. The map gives you a route, and autopilot makes the game accessible to people who do not play driving games.” |
| 30–42 s | Enter the first mission marker. Let the short introduction finish. | “First energy bill. The mission puts the lesson on the road, where it becomes a decision.” |
| 42–60 s | Use **Drive for me** to follow the earlier movers and collect the three hindsight pickups. | “Follow people ahead. Collect what helped them. The crowd evidence stays anonymous and only appears when the group is large enough.” |
| 60–72 s | Choose **A dated meter photo on moving-in day**, then **Drive for me** to cross the bump. | “Prepare before the bump. A dated meter reading helps separate usage before and after moving day.” |
| 72–82 s | Show **LESSON PASSED**, the passport stamp and **Ground floor built**. Save the reminder if offered. | “The reward is understanding. A passed mission improves your block. A practical reminder is optional.” |
| 82–90 s | Open pause and return to the app. Show the Home mission progress. | “The useful step comes back with you. Margaret can read the same lesson plainly. Nadia gets support at her pace, without a game.” |

## Recovery and repeat

- If you take a wrong turn, open the full map and use autopilot to the mission marker.
- If the preparation is wrong, use **Rewind 5 s**. Nothing is lost.
- To restart the game, open pause with **Esc**, then select **Reset demo**.
- To restart from Home, open `/?p=tom&reset=1` again.
- If the projector cannot run WebGL, use **Open plain lessons** on the startup screen and explain the same preparation step.
- Do not rush through all three missions. The 90-second run demonstrates one complete loop and leaves room to explore.
